# Future Work

This project opens several opportunities for future research and development.

1. Integrate Explainable AI techniques such as SHAP and LIME to improve
model transparency and interpretability.

2. Expand evaluation to larger CKD datasets collected from multiple
healthcare institutions.

3. Develop a real-time adaptive learning framework capable of continuously
updating itself from patient streams.

4. Deploy the model as a web application to support clinicians during
screening and diagnosis.

5. Extend the framework to additional diseases including cardiovascular
disease, liver disease, and breast cancer.

6. Investigate advanced deep learning architectures and concept drift
detection methods such as ADWIN and DDM.

7. Conduct prospective studies to evaluate clinical effectiveness in
real-world healthcare environments.

The long-term objective is to build a general-purpose adaptive healthcare
assistant capable of supporting early disease detection across multiple
medical domains.
